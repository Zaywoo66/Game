# DECISIONS (log + open questions)

## Open
- D4 Windows x64 is the current test platform. The first PC has a verified Tauri launch and GPU measurement. The second player's GPU and FPS and a one-hour LAN session on two physical Windows PCs remain open. Same-PC runs verify loopback behavior.
- D8 Disconnect policy. ASSUMED: hold the country for a grace period, then the host may enable temporary AI. M5 exposes a host-selected 0–60 minute grace, initially 2 minutes. Resume tokens last for the running guest process and reserve its seat for the room lifetime. A permanent guest app exit requires recreating the room. This remains a prototype policy for review.

- D10 AI difficulty. ASSUMED: no cheats by default. Bonuses require explicit modifiers.

- D14 Far-region countries as stub countries first. ASSUMED: yes.

## Decided
- M6 alliance: indefinite; either party may break during peace or war, losing 25 mutual opinion points (clamped to -100..100). A new offer between the same pair is blocked until two Gregorian calendar months pass, clamping the day to the final day of the target month. Offers expire after 30 game days. Direct wartime enemies cannot ally; declaring war on an active ally requires an explicit break first and returns a typed reason otherwise. A postwar truce does not block alliance. Humans can accept despite negative opinion. At negative opinion, AI accepts only with a shared stronger land neighbor, then with 10% deterministic chance. The shared neighbor borders both parties by normal or river edge and has strictly more total active soldiers than each; this leaves the one-war-per-country M4 model unchanged.
- D1 Time mode: real-time with pause. Each tick advances one day in the proleptic Gregorian calendar.
- D2 Time controls: either human player may pause or resume. Only the host sets speed.
- D0 Stack: Rust sim/net, Tauri 2, React/TS UI, three.js map. Fallback if WebView/WebGL fails: Godot.
- D3 Network: host-authoritative with deltas; lockstep deferred.
- D5 First vertical-slice region: Western Europe and the British Isles, with 5–8 countries. Slice target ~400–600 provinces (supersedes the ~100-province target, which was too coarse). Confirm density in M5b and record the measurements here.
- D6 Graphics: 3D terrain + low-poly instanced units, 2D UI; gameplay is province-graph based.
- D7 Scope: beta starts in 1440 and covers Europe, the Mediterranean and the Near East, with content to ~1500. Density follows MAP_SCALE.md. The world map follows the beta in M13.

- D11 AI lives in the `ai` crate, runs on the host, and acts through validated commands. It uses utility scoring, data-driven profiles, and an RNG derived from `(seed, tick, country_id)`.

- D12 A province is a settlement and its surroundings. The hierarchy is province → area → region (MAP_SCALE.md).

- D15 Diplomatic opinion is directional, in signed whole points from -100 through 100. Unspecified relations start at 0; changes clamp at the bounds. There is no passive decay until a separate mechanic defines it.

- D16 1440 steppe ownership follows the owner-provided map with one Great Horde for the region labelled “Golden Horde” (treated as a legacy map label), a separate Nogai Horde, an eastern Uzbek Khanate extended through Tura, and a separate Crimean Khanate. Province ownership uses the approximate bands and explicit overrides in `data/map/europe/ownership_1440.json`; the image is a regional reference, not a source of exact polygon geometry. Wakkas-biy, Sayyid Ahmad, Abu’l-Khayr and Hacı I Giray use game birth-year estimates where documented in their character records.
- D17 Country ownership: the Holy Roman Empire is a confederation, not a single owner; member states remain distinct. The Kalmar label is not a unified country owner: Denmark, Norway and Sweden remain separate campaign countries; Iceland has no provinces in the current map raster. Scandinavian cells are split by the approximate, owner-approved rule in `data/map/europe/ownership_1440.json`. (Owner clarification, 2026-11-13.)
