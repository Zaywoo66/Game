### Local 1440 political-reference review (draft alpha content)

The owner supplied the installed Age of Civilizations II 1440 scenario as an
additional political reference. `tools/map/audit_aoc2_1440.py` reads that
installation locally and writes a temporary comparison CSV; its proprietary
geometry, serialized scenario files, and CSV are not redistributed. The
crosswalk used 513 matching city controls (2.62 game-pixel RMS). Among 1,284
land cells, 953 had at least 75% one-owner sample agreement before the stricter
eight-sample filter; 240 were mixed and 91 unmatched. This is game-reference
evidence, not a historical border survey.

`tools/map/add_1440_minor_polities.py` records the reviewed country crosswalk
and adds 27 AI minor polities (including the sourced Palatinate and free city
of Schaffhausen). Their 83 land cells include conservative local-reference
transfers and individually reviewed city corrections. The scenario now has 72
countries and all 1,284 land cells still have exactly one owner. Minor countries without a documented 1440 ruler use
`ruler: null`; their capitals and AI profiles remain required. Abstract
original banners are listed in `ASSETS.md`. Capitals such as Berezniki for
Perm and Tartu for the Livonian Order are game-map proxies, not claims about
historical seats. The ownership policy stores reviewed overrides so a map
regeneration preserves them; still-mixed HRE cells retain marked gameplay
approximations.

The local game's polygon vote is not authoritative for named cities. Dresden
is assigned to Saxony using the [Saxony government history](https://publikationen.sachsen.de/bdb/artikel/10797/documents/10914)
and the [Library of Congress Wettin account](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/dres/dresremb.html).
Freiburg remains Austrian: the [city's history](https://www.freiburg.de/pb/site/Freiburg/get/documents_E-541455541/freiburg/daten/news/amtsblatt/pdf/Sonderdruck_Revue2020.pdf)
dates its Habsburg allegiance from 1368. Heidelberg is the Palatinate capital
per [UNESCO's historical account](https://whc.unesco.org/archive/2007/whc07-31com-inf8b1e.pdf),
and Schaffhausen is a separate imperial city after regaining freedom in 1415
per its [official city chronology](https://www.stadt-schaffhausen.ch/index.php/geschichtesw/28874).
The game-reference Baden grouping was therefore removed rather than copied.
Cologne is a separate archbishopric with its capital cell supported by 6/6
local samples; the [archdiocesan list](https://www.erzbistum-koeln.de/erzbistum/erzbistum_im_ueberblick/geschichte_zeitstrahl/verzeichnis_koelner_erzbischoefe/)
names Dietrich II von Moers for 1414–1463, although the game character remains
unset pending a sourced birth date. Belfast (5/5) and Lisburn (7/7) form a
playable Ulster approximation, not a claim of a unified 1440 state. These two
small-polity exceptions require unanimous sample votes and a minimum of five
samples.

Bremen (`brm`) and Hamburg (`ham`) now own only their named city cells, as
explicit playable municipal approximations. They do not receive surrounding
territory from the mixed AoC2 samples. Bremen's council had substantial
autonomy, while formal recognition as a Free Imperial City came in 1646 per
the [UNESCO/ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/5302). Hamburg's
late-medieval legal position was contested: the [University of Hamburg's
medieval project](https://www.spaetmittelalter.uni-hamburg.de/spaetmittelalter/Lehre/Ergebnisse/Hamburg/themen/StadtUndLandesherrschaft.html)
places formal imperial immediacy in 1510; its discussion of [city law and
autonomy](https://www.spaetmittelalter.uni-hamburg.de/spaetmittelalter/Lehre/Ergebnisse/Hamburg/themen/EntwicklungDesHamburgerStadtrechts.html)
shows municipal self-government earlier. A city-level cell owner is therefore
a campaign simplification, not a claim that either city had uncontested
sovereign Reichsstadt status on 1 January 1440. The policy pins only `bremen`
to `brm` and `hamburg` to `ham`; their ruler fields remain null.
